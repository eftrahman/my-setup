# Third-party sources

The desktop configuration is derived from JaKooLit's Hyprland-Dots and retains its GPL-3.0 license in `LICENSE`. Fresh Ubuntu installation is delegated to LinuxBeginnings/Ubuntu-Hyprland, the announced maintained successor.

Runtime components are fetched from their official projects rather than vendored: Oh My Zsh, Powerlevel10k, zsh-autosuggestions, zsh-syntax-highlighting, Oh My Tmux, NVM, Nerd Fonts, and Powerlevel10k media. Exact audited Git revisions and the Nerd Fonts release are recorded in `config/versions.env`.

Wallpaper and icon assets retain their original authors' licenses. Keep the repository private unless you have confirmed redistribution rights for every visual asset.
